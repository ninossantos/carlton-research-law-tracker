# International coercive-control instruments — official-source verification

Verified 31 August 2026 (PT). Named-term test: does the **statute text** use the words **coercive control** or the UK phrase **controlling or coercive behaviour**? Adjacent words (coerce or control; controlling, regulating or monitoring; coercive and controlling behaviour) are noted but do not pass the named-term test unless one of those two phrases appears.

Method: government or legislation sites. Secondary roundups are not the source. If a live fetch failed, the row says so. Nothing invented.
